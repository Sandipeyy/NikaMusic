# NikaMusic

A free music app for Android. Search any song, get mixes made for you, sing along with synced lyrics, keep your favorites offline and listen together with friends. No account needed.

**[Download the latest version](../../releases/latest)** · **[Website](https://sandipeyy.github.io/nikamusic/)**

## Features
- Search with live suggestions, recent searches and a top result
- Mixes made for you: Daily Mixes, Discover, On repeat and a mix for the time of day
- Follow artists and get songs from them on Home
- Synced lyrics with timing buttons, shared as text
- Offline downloads, auto-download of liked songs on Wi-Fi, save MP3 to your phone
- Listen together: share what is playing and send song requests
- Home screen widget with the album poster, and notification controls
- Extensions: add your own music source with just a link
- Backup and restore, listening stats and a recap page
- Update check and What's new inside the app

## Install
1. Open the [latest release](../../releases/latest) on your phone and download `NikaMusic.apk`.
2. Allow your browser or file manager to install apps when Android asks.
3. Open the file, tap Install, then pick a few artists you like.

If Android says "App not installed", uninstall the old NikaMusic once and install the new file. Back up your library first in Settings.

## Privacy
NikaMusic has no accounts and no analytics. Your library, playlists and history stay on your phone. Reports are only sent if you choose to send them.
