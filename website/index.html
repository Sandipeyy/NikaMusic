What it does

A music player made for listening, not for setting up.

Open it, pick a few artists you like, and Home fills with music that fits you.

Search with suggestions

Songs, albums and artists, with suggestions as you type and your recent searches one tap away.

Mixes made for you

Daily Mixes, Discover, On repeat and a mix for the time of day, built from what you play and the artists you follow.

Synced lyrics

Lines light up as the song plays. Tap a line to jump there, nudge the timing if it is off, or share a line.

Offline downloads

Save songs, auto-download your liked songs on Wi-Fi, and save an MP3 to your phone.

Listen together

Join a room with a friend and share what is playing, and send each other song requests.

Widget and notification

Control playback from the home screen widget with the album poster, and from the notification.

Extensions

Add your own stream sources with a small JSON description. No code from an extension is ever run.

Backup and updates

Back up your library to a file and restore it on a new phone. The app tells you when a new version is out.

Install

How to install NikaMusic on Android.

Download

Get the APK

Tap Download for Android. On the GitHub page, open the latest release and tap NikaMusic.apk.

Allow

Let your browser install apps

Android asks once. Turn on "Allow from this source" for the browser or file manager you used.

Open

Install and pick artists

Open the file, tap Install, then choose a few artists you like so Home starts with music for you.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Is it on the Play Store?

No. NikaMusic is an APK you download from GitHub, so you install it yourself. Android shows a one-time permission prompt for that.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. The builds are for Android only.

Where does the music come from?

Search, posters and details come from Deezer. Full songs are matched from several open streaming sources, and if a full song is not found, the app can play a 30 second preview. What is available can change from day to day.

I see "App not installed". What now?

That happens when a new version was signed with a different key than the one on your phone. Uninstall the old NikaMusic once, then install the new file. Back up your library first in Settings.

Do I need an account?

No. Your library, playlists and listening history stay on your phone. Use Backup and restore to move them.

Ready when you are.

Free, no account, and it keeps working when you are offline with your downloads.
