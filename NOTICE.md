# NikaMusic — attribution

NikaMusic is a modified fork of **Mochy Music** (https://github.com/realidkroo/Mochy-Music),
which is itself built on other open-source music apps (see the Mochy README history and the
credits screen in the app: Metrolist, ViVi Music, SimpMusic, InnerTune, RiMusic, ArchiveTune and others).

All of it is licensed under the **GNU General Public License v3.0** (see `LICENSE`).
As a derivative work, NikaMusic must stay under GPL-3.0 and its source must be made available
to anyone who receives the app. Original copyright notices must not be removed.

Changes in this fork: renamed to NikaMusic (`com.sandip.nikamusic`), new logo/icons,
upstream updater/changelog endpoints repointed, startup intro animation disabled.

## Code ported from Echo Music

Parts of NikaMusic are adapted from **Echo Music** (https://github.com/iad1tya/Echo-Music, GPL-3.0):
the music-recognition Quick Settings tile and notification service, the "set as ringtone" dialogs,
and the home-screen widget skins (Modern Flow, Compact Glow, Showcase Card, Slim Strip, Vinyl Square,
Vintage Turntable). Each ported file keeps a header naming its origin.
